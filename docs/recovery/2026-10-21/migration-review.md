## Operational and compatibility risks

- ALTER TABLE and ordinary CREATE INDEX acquire locks. Foreign-key validation/index creation may scan existing tables. No measured rehearsal duration guarantees zero blocking under real concurrent traffic; briefly quiesce Control writes for the approved operation and set lock/statement timeouts rather than waiting indefinitely.
- New nullable fields/defaulted fields support old records. The old application does not know new quote states; keep traffic on a compatible release and do not create new quote/task/product data with an older binary after rollback.
- A failed non-transactional migration may leave partial objects. Stop on any error, inspect its ledger/DDL, and follow `docs/production-recovery.md`; do not mark migrations applied, retry blindly, or alter migration checksums.
- Preview currently has zero business records, three NumberSequence rows and 13 ledger rows. Empty business tables do **not** prove historical invoice preservation in a populated Production database. Financial/media preservation must also be rehearsed with representative synthetic pre-migration rows on the restored isolated copy. No conclusion here authorizes Production migration.
- The child's inherited login can access its own branch; scripts assert the precise child host before any write. The real Preview host is excluded from every writable rehearsal guard. SMTP is disabled in the lab.

## Backup scope

The verified target advertises a six-hour history window; it had no listed explicit snapshots at inspection. This is not a long-term backup guarantee. An independent private `pg_dump` of its pre-migration child and a successful local restore with exact schema/count/sequence comparison are required evidence.
